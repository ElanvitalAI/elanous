# Elanous trademark policy (draft)

## S — What this covers

Elanous is the product name; its wordmark and logo identify the official product. This policy covers the names **Elanous** and **엘라누스**, the Elanous wordmark, the Elanous logo/icon and confusingly similar branding used to identify software or services. It is a policy for identifying the source of a distribution, not a restriction on using the source code.

## C — Why the distinction matters

A fork can use the code without being an official Elanous release. Keeping the original product name and logo on a separately maintained fork would make its origin, maintenance and support ambiguous. Conversely, removing truthful attribution would hide the origin of code that the fork is entitled to reuse.

## Q — How can a fork identify its origin without presenting itself as official?

## A — Use of the marks

- **Rename and rebrand independently distributed forks.** Before offering a fork under your own maintenance, change its public-facing product name and logo/icon to distinct ones. This includes the repository or package identity, app name and icon, website, installer, UI and marketing material where they present the fork as a product. Do not distribute a fork as “Elanous,” with the Elanous logo, or in a way that suggests an official release, sponsorship, certification or support from Elanvital AI. A fork's code may retain identifiers needed for compatibility and factual history; this is not permission to brand that fork as Elanous.
- **Attribution and compatibility are allowed.** You may accurately say that your separately named project is a fork of Elanous, link to the original project and describe interoperability with it, without implying affiliation. Keep the copyright, attribution and other notices required by [LICENSE](LICENSE), including relevant [NOTICE](NOTICE) text; rebranding does not erase those obligations. Such notices need not be replaced with the fork's new product name, and preserving them does not make the fork official.
- **“Powered by Elanous” is a bounded origin statement.** You may use those exact words in ordinary text to describe software genuinely built on or incorporating Elanous code, alongside the fork's own distinct name, with a clear link or attribution to the original project and a visible statement that the fork is independently maintained and is not endorsed by Elanvital AI. Do not use the phrase as the fork's name, logo, primary badge or a claim of official partnership, approval, certification, current compatibility, or support. Do not use the Elanous logo with that phrase without separate written permission. If the code relationship is no longer accurate, stop using the statement.
- **Other promotional uses require permission.** Use of the marks beyond truthful origin/compatibility references and the bounded statement above, including co-branding or using the official logo as your project's identity, requires separate written permission from Elanvital AI. This policy does not promise such permission.
