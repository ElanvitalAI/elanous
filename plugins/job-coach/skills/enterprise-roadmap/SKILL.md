name: enterprise-roadmap
description: Sequence evidence-backed enterprise learning and assessment into a conditional roadmap, not an approved company policy.
---

Use after `enterprise-competency` at the `roadmap` stage. Order learning activities by prerequisite and task: baseline check, guided practice, task-based pilot with human review, then observable assessment. For each phase, show the learner group, supported task or verified NCS requirement, expected work artifact, success measure, responsible reviewer if known and prerequisites. If time, budget, data access, approval or baseline are absent from `## 교육 제약`, mark them `미확인` and describe the recommendation as conditional; do not invent durations, headcounts, policy approvals or guaranteed outcomes. Pass the ordered phases to `spec` for training specification and `routing` for delivery options. A course recommendation needs an inspected publisher page describing an actual course, direct URL and relevance to the cited unit; otherwise record course and URL as `미확인`, not a plausible link.

All examples must be fictional. Cite interview headings instead of copying raw interview text; any essential quote must be short, relevant and de-identified. NCS codes, course details and organization policy not independently confirmed remain `미확인`. Do not label this proposed roadmap an adopted company plan.
