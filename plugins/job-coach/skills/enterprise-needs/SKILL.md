---
name: enterprise-needs
description: Extract evidence-backed organizational learning needs from a fictional enterprise interview without claiming that a requested course is the solution.
---

Use after `interview-to-profile` in enterprise mode (`intake` → `needs`). Read the enterprise interview headings `## 기업 개요`, `## 교육 대상`, `## 현업 과제`, `## 교육 목표`, `## 현재 역량`, `## 교육 제약`. Distinguish what the organization requested from what its workflow evidence supports. For each need, record the affected learner group, task or bottleneck, desired observable outcome, evidence section and missing information. Keep unsupported causes and target levels `미확인`; ask for missing evidence rather than invent a baseline or learner count. A request for AI training is not proof that AI is the best intervention.

Use only fictional employer and learner details in examples. Summarize or cite interview sections instead of reproducing interview paragraphs; if an excerpt is essential, quote only a short relevant fragment without names or identifiers. Never print the raw interview or credentials into graph stdout. Company approval and data-use policy, NCS mappings, named courses and course URLs remain `미확인` until independently verified. Hand needs and unresolved questions to `task`, not a prematurely selected course.
