name: enterprise-ai-fit
description: Assess AI applicability to verified enterprise tasks, including non-AI alternatives and policy unknowns.
---

Use at the enterprise `ai-fit` stage after `task` and the existing `ncs-match` stage. For each concrete task, compare a plausible AI-assisted workflow with a non-AI alternative. Record the input data type, expected benefit, failure mode, human review point, testable outcome and evidence from the task or confirmed NCS unit. Separate suitability from a promise of productivity gain. If data rights, access, privacy, security or organization approval are not documented, mark policy and deployment feasibility `미확인`; do not assert permission to upload internal information to an external model. When AI adds no justified value or a task is insufficiently described, recommend the non-AI or evidence-gathering route rather than an AI course.

Use only fictional company examples. Summarize the interview instead of copying quotations; any indispensable quotation must be brief, relevant and de-identified. Never fabricate an NCS code, course name, URL or internal policy; mark unknown details `미확인`. Hand the supported AI-fit and unresolved constraints to `competency`.
