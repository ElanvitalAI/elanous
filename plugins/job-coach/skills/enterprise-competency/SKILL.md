name: enterprise-competency
description: Compare task and verified NCS requirements to self-reported enterprise competencies without inferring incapacity.
---

Use after `enterprise-ai-fit` at the `competency` stage. Map each verified NCS unit's official code, name, level and criteria to the relevant task, keeping its source. Compare those requirements with `## 현재 역량` as self-report only. Classify each requirement as evidenced by interview, gap in evidence, or `미확인` where baseline evidence is absent; never equate missing evidence with inability or claim that an interview verifies mastery. For AI-suitable tasks, include human review, data handling and evaluation skills only insofar as the task and evidence require them. Link each proposed learning outcome to a task and an observable assessment; flag unverified NCS detail `미확인`, not an invented official unit.

Use fictional people and employers in examples. Do not persist interview quotations or confidential descriptions; at most use a short, de-identified excerpt when necessary for a specific finding. Course availability and URL, approval to use company data and applicable company policy remain `미확인` until checked. Send the evidence-backed competencies and uncertainties to `roadmap`.
