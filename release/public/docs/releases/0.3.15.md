# Elanous 0.3.15

This release brings more of your work into one connected flow, from saving an article to acting on it in your own workspace.

- Open a recommendation link using your member ID and continue in your own view.
- Send an article to the Telegram bot to save it as a scrap and use it in later work.
- Connect the Android companion app to your account and share content into Elanous.
- Record a voice note and return to it alongside your other saved material.
- Keep separate conversations and tools within reach using side tabs.

## Known issues

Some PWA flows may still be unreliable, and the full automated test sweep did not complete.
