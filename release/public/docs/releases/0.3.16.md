# 0.3.16

This release separates your public member ID from the secret keys that sign you in, and carries that line across every place you use Elanous: the web, Telegram, Android, iPhone and the terminal.

- Share Elanous with a link that carries only your public member ID. Sign-in sessions, app tokens and connection codes stay out of links and screens.
- See your public member ID in the Android and iPhone apps once you are signed in, and keep it current when your session changes.
- On iPhone, your sign-in session is now kept in the secure Keychain, and items you share are sent in order and only after the previous one is confirmed.
- On Android, a pasted Mac connection link is hidden as you type, and drafts you share stay saved until Elanous confirms them.
- In Telegram, an article you send to the bot is saved before it waits its turn, so it is not lost if Elanous restarts. Referral replies show your public member ID.
- Voice notes keep their transcript and can be retried if sending a saved recording fails.
- In the terminal, stored secrets are no longer printed to the screen unless you ask for them explicitly.

## Known issues

The full automated test gate did not complete. Some PWA flows may still be unreliable.
